Partnership opportunities

We work as an extension of your leadership team.

Healthcare technology is a team sport, and the organizations that need enterprise architecture most are often the ones least able to staff it. Our partnership model is built for that gap: senior architecture leadership that joins your table, understands your constraints and stays through implementation. Here is how that looks across the organizations we serve.

Community & rural hospitals

Standing alongside the hospitals that are often the only source of care.

A dedicated partnership model for hospitals facing distance, workforce shortages, thin margins and the same regulatory burden as systems ten times their size.

Right-sized modernization

Sustainable technology investments that fit a rural or community hospital's budget and staffing reality, not enterprise solutions built for much larger systems.

Care coordination & interoperability

Connecting hospitals to regional systems, specialists and exchanges on open standards, so patients are not lost between referrals.

Specialist access & telehealth

Technology pathways that extend scarce specialists to patients who otherwise could not reach them.

Transformation funding alignment

Aligning technology roadmaps to state and federal rural health transformation programs so investments are fundable as well as sensible.

Health systems, physician groups & FQHCs

Interoperability, workflow and the platforms behind new care models.

  • Health systems & ACOs

    Portfolio governance, EHR integration and optimization, trusted exchange participation, value-based care data strategy and the care-management platforms that new payment models require. We are comfortable inside large, politically complex organizations and know how to get decisions made.

  • Physician groups, MSOs & independent practices

    Launching and scaling care-management and complex-care programs: platform selection, revenue-stack modeling, remote monitoring integration and the operating model to run the program profitably. Fractional CIO/CTO support for groups that have outgrown their IT vendor but cannot justify an executive hire.

  • FQHCs & safety-net clinics

    Grant-aligned technology roadmaps, interoperability with the hospitals and HIEs around you, health-related social needs data, and program eligibility and navigation tools for the populations you serve.

  • Home-based & post-acute care

    Architecture for care delivered where people live: mobile and remote workflows, monitoring data, caregiver access and the integration back into the primary record.

Payers & health plans

Compliance deadlines turned into durable data-exchange capability.

  • Interoperability & prior authorization compliance

    Target architecture and delivery planning for Patient Access, Provider Access, Payer-to-Payer and Prior Authorization APIs, including vendor evaluation, data mapping and the operational metrics reporting the rules require.

  • Medicare Advantage & Medicaid managed care

    Member and caregiver data access, supplemental benefit and care-management program integration, and the data strategy for quality, risk and value-based provider contracts.

  • Payer–provider collaboration

    Shared data architectures for attribution, gap closure and care coordination that both sides can trust, built on standard implementation guides rather than bespoke feeds.

Public sector, HIEs & program offices

Program architecture, governance and trusted exchange for the public mission.

  • Federal & state health programs

    Enterprise and solution architecture for program systems, legacy migration planning, architecture review support and the governance operating models that keep large portfolios coherent. Available as a prime, subcontractor or teaming partner.

  • State HIEs & health data utilities

    Trusted exchange readiness and onboarding, participant governance, identity and consent architecture, public-health reporting and the sustainability models that keep exchanges funded.

  • Public health & social services agencies

    Data-sharing architectures across health and social care, eligibility and navigation tools, and the privacy posture that community data demands.

  • Rural health transformation programs

    Technical assistance and architecture support for state transformation initiatives and the hospitals, clinics and partners funded through them.

Health technology companies

Product strategy and reference architecture for companies selling into healthcare.

  • Digital health startups

    Fractional CTO and product architecture: standards-based reference architecture, identity and consent design, data-source strategy, certification and marketplace pathways, and the regulatory fluency to answer a health system's security questionnaire the first time.

  • Interoperability & platform vendors

    Implementation-guide conformance, trusted exchange participation strategy, customer-facing architecture support and credible thought leadership for your market.

  • Investors & advisors

    Technical and regulatory diligence on healthcare technology companies: architecture quality, interoperability posture, compliance exposure and the realism of the payment path.

Universities & research institutions

Translating research into deployable health technology.

  • Joint funding pursuits

    As a registered small business, we can serve as the commercial partner on small-business innovation and technology transfer awards, bringing the architecture, market and regulatory sides that academic teams often need.

  • Applied research platforms

    Architecture for research data platforms, consented data access, survey and cohort systems and the migration of legacy research tooling to governed cloud environments.

  • Student & faculty collaboration

    Capstone projects, sponsored research and practitioner teaching on healthcare interoperability, enterprise architecture and health technology product development.

How every partnership works

Listen, co-design, phase, stay.

The sequence is the same regardless of partner type. Only the weight changes.

  1. Listen & assess

    We start with leadership and the teams doing the work to understand the mission, the constraints and the barriers standing in the way.

  2. Co-design the approach

    Roadmaps are designed with you, not for you, so the plan fits your realities, staff capacity and funding.

  3. Implement in phases

    Change arrives in increments the organization can absorb without disrupting operations or patient care.

  4. Support for the long term

    Our involvement does not end at go-live. We stay engaged as a long-term architecture partner, so strategic support is always on hand.
